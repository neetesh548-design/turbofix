## Slide 6: Pillar 2 — Verified Repair Closure & 5-Why Safety

### 📺 Visual Layout (4-Step Verification Card)
1. **Assigned Ticket Queue**: Technician receives direct work order with parts checklist.
2. **Mandatory LOTO Safety Check**: Lock-Out Tag-Out safety verification before starting hydraulic/electrical work.
3. **Photo Proof of Fix**: Technician must capture clear photo/video evidence of replaced spare or repair.
4. **Supervisor Sign-off & 5-Why RCA**: Machine restarts only after supervisor verifies root cause.

### 🗣️ Presenter Script (Talk Track)
> *"No ticket can be closed by simply saying 'Fixed'. Technicians must upload photo proof of the completed repair. For major breakdowns, TurboFix guides the team through a 5-Why Root Cause Analysis: Why did the bearing fail? Lack of lubrication. Why? Oiler tube clogged. Why? Cleaning PM missed. By identifying the true root cause, we stop repeat breakdowns permanently."*
